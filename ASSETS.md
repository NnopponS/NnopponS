# Profile visuals and sources

The README banner (`assets/innoserve-stage-2026.jpg`) is a photograph supplied by Worapon Sangsasri from the InnoServe Awards 2026 ceremony, where WheelSense placed third in International Exchange (English). It was cropped and compressed for GitHub, with its capture metadata removed. The photograph has not been generated or retouched.

Profile facts are aligned with the [LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/worapon-sangsasri-0371a8376/), the [public competition recap](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7531009753288691712/), the [portfolio](https://nnoppons.github.io/), and the public project repositories linked in the README. Team achievements are credited to the team.

The Field Notes banner, project cards, and navigation badges are original vector drawings, with no remote assets. The banner includes a decorative signal animation and honors the operating system's reduced-motion setting. Its waveform is illustrative, not recorded data. Regenerate the Field Notes SVGs with `python scripts/build_field_assets.py`.

Older custom SVG/GIF assets and the generator under `scripts/` remain in the repository for reference. They are not served by the profile README. Keep `README.md` at the root of this public profile repository.
